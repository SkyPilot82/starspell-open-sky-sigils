Why the ground is crisp now. The blur was the cost of surgery: frame A1's ground is woven from light — lamp chains sit ON the hills' contour lines, fireflies hide IN the grass — so cutting the lights out in post kept smearing the paint no matter how carefully the holes were filled. The honest fix was to go back to Midjourney: same frozen formula, same anchor, with the ground born unlit (--no fireflies, lanterns, village lights). This frame IS that roll — dark layered hills, crisp edges, zero surgery. Its three siblings are below; A1-original and A1-surgical stay linked for comparison.

The sky, per your notes: the stars twinkle deeper and quicker now (most dots breathe from bright to near-gone, on 0.9–2.2s cycles); still 44 dots + 6 heroes. And a shooting star's flight is now clamped above the ridge — the whole crossing, head and trail, ends before the ground line, every time. Cadence stays ~3.5–7s with the first inside 5s.

Before it touches the game: on your SEND, the build card carries the 512×768 cut of this frame plus exactly these living-layer dials (field, cadence, twinkle depth, above-the-ridge clamp). Nothing ships until you say so.

How they fit. Each row is the MJ banner itself — the frame painted at the row's own shape, the icon roundel and the real game text laid into its dark panel exactly where the current baked chrome puts them. On a build, each frame is cut off its painted backdrop and composited on the game's navy, so the rows sit on the screen the way today's gold ones do; the roundel and text stay live text, never baked in.

The rarity story at a glance: a common's quiet wood, a rare's cool crystal, a legendary arriving like an ember — unmistakable down a stack of three, which is the entire job of rarity dress.

IV · THREE QUESTIONS

Q1 · THE LEGENDARY COLOR. You called legendary glowing orange, and the plaque above wears it. In the game today, legendary dress is gold everywhere — the drop card's gold rays, the grade-IV ink, the award fanfare. Does orange become the legendary color of the whole game, or does gold keep the game and orange live only on these rectangles?

Q2 · WHAT DO THE RECTANGLES DRESS? Common→rare→epic→legendary is the ladder a held sigil climbs (I·II·III·IV — EPIC lives only there today; drops fall as basic / rare / legendary). Are these four rectangles the dress of the grades, of the drops (which would add an epic drop tier), or both?

Q3 · SEND THE OPEN SKY TO THE GAME? The hero card above is your 9/13 spec — ground dark below the red line, twinkle field ×2, crossings ~2× as often. On SEND, the build card carries the darkened plate + those exact living-layer dials (art swap + v0.84.0 dial raise, nothing else).

V · WHAT A BUILD WOULD COST (honest)

The open sky: small. Cut the picked frame to 512×768 like the zodiac set (~40KB), load it exactly as the twelve signed cards load theirs, one version bump; the living layer, the census pins and the ?twinkle=0 seam are already shipped and untouched. ~half a day with suite re-proof.

The rows: the shape is right, so the work is the cut, not the re-proportioning: each frame comes off its painted backdrop with an authored mask (never a color key) and composites onto the game's navy; the roundel and text stay live, and the pick sheet's rows are fixed-size, so no slicing is needed there. Other surfaces (forge, sign pages, the versus inspector) size differently — per-surface exports, or edge slices for the simple frames; the legendary's glow, sparks and shimmer are re-added in-engine, since baked light on a scaled sprite peels like a sticker. ~1.5–2 days for the family.

Orange-everywhere (if Q1 says so): recolor of the legendary dress + fanfare + suites re-pinned, ~half a day on top.
